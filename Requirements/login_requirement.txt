Feature: Modern Ecommerce Product Search and Bag Validation

URL:
https://www.myntra.com/

Search Keyword:
black shirts for men

Preferred Size:
40

Description:

Validate product search, product selection, size selection, Add to Bag,
bag-item verification, and product removal in the Myntra ecommerce
application.

The test must use only controls and elements that are available in the
application.

The framework must not generate login, username, password, logout,
registration, or authentication steps.

The framework must not invent locators, URLs, products, sizes, prices,
messages, or application behavior.

Positive Test Steps:

STEP-001
Launch the Myntra ecommerce application using the supplied URL.

STEP-002
Wait until the home page is ready.

STEP-003
Verify that the application header or Myntra logo is displayed.

STEP-004
Verify that the product search field is displayed.

STEP-005
Enter the configured Search Keyword in the product search field.

STEP-006
Press the Enter key to perform the search.

STEP-007
Wait until the search-results page is displayed.

STEP-008
Verify that the current URL or page state represents the search results.

STEP-009
Verify that the searched-product list or product-results container is displayed.

STEP-010
Verify that at least one product is displayed in the search results.

STEP-011
Select the first available product from the search results.

STEP-012
If the selected product opens in a new browser tab, switch to the newly opened tab.

STEP-013
Wait until the product-details page is displayed.

STEP-014
Verify that the selected product name is displayed.

STEP-015
Verify that the selected product price is displayed.

STEP-016
Verify that the available-size options are displayed.

STEP-017
Select size 40 only if size 40 is available for the selected product.

STEP-018
If size 40 is unavailable, select the first available size and record the selected size for later validation.

STEP-019
Verify that the Add to Bag button is displayed.

STEP-020
Click the Add to Bag button.

STEP-021
Wait until the bag state or item count is updated.

STEP-022
Verify that the bag icon or bag link is displayed.

STEP-023
Verify that the bag item count is greater than zero, if an item-count indicator is available.

STEP-024
Click the bag icon or bag link.

STEP-025
Wait until the shopping-bag page is displayed.

STEP-026
Verify that the previously selected product is displayed in the bag.

STEP-027
Verify that the bag displays the selected product name.

STEP-028
Verify that the bag displays the selected size.

STEP-029
Verify that the product price is displayed in the bag.

STEP-030
Verify that the Remove action is displayed for the selected product.

STEP-031
Click the Remove action.

STEP-032
Confirm the removal if the application displays a confirmation dialog.

STEP-033
Wait until the selected product is removed from the bag.

STEP-034
Verify that the selected product is no longer displayed in the bag.

STEP-035
Verify that the bag item count is zero, hidden, or updated appropriately.
